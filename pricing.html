Frequently Asked Questions

What kind of tricky life situations does CasePath handle?

CasePath is engineered for any complex life event requiring structured fact-finding and legal review: workplace termination or disciplinary actions, encounters with police or regulatory bodies, landlord/tenant disputes, contract breaches, council enforcement notices, consumer disputes, and personal/family conflicts.

Is CasePath providing legal advice?

No. CasePath is an objective information-organisation and document-preparation workbench. It does not provide legal representation, does not form a solicitor-client relationship, and does not determine liability or court outcomes. Its purpose is to turn your raw account into a professional, clear record so your chosen lawyer can advise you efficiently.

Where is my data stored?

In private draft mode, your story, documents, and generated outputs reside on your local device within this browser. No unencrypted case data is broadcast or sold. When you export, you receive a cryptographically signed package that only you and your lawyer control.

What is the difference between the Preparation Pass and the Handover Bundle?

The Preparation Pass gives you full chronological ordering, evidence gap identification, and the standard Lawyer Brief. The Matter Handover Bundle includes the formal Chained Accountability Correspondence (request letters for missing CCTV, personnel files, or authority logs), the cryptographically hashed Evidence Vault, and the complete audit derivation receipts.
