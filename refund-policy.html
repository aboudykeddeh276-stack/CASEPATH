Refund Policy

How CasePath handles subscription refunds, cancellations, billing disputes, and Australian Consumer Law rights.

Information only — not legal advice.

At a glance: This Refund Policy explains how CasePath handles subscription refunds, cancellations, billing disputes, and Australian Consumer Law rights. It does not provide legal advice about your individual circumstances.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Overview

CasePath is an Australian family-law preparation platform. Paid access may include monthly or annual subscriptions, document credits, and one-time products such as preparation packs. Payments are processed by Stripe.

This policy should be read together with our Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, and Pricing pages. If there is an inconsistency on consumer guarantees that cannot be excluded by law, Australian Consumer Law prevails.

CasePath provides legal information and document assistance software only. It does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or guaranteed court outcomes.

2. Subscription refunds

Unless a statutory consumer guarantee or other non-excludable right applies:

  • Subscription fees are generally non-refundable for unused time after a billing period has started.
  • If you cancel a subscription, access continues until the end of the current paid billing period. You will not be charged for the next period after cancellation takes effect.
  • Where CasePath cannot provide a paid feature you purchased because of a verified platform fault on our side, we will offer a remedy that may include a repair, replacement access period, or refund of the affected charge, as appropriate.
  • One-time purchases (for example document credits or fixed-fee packs) are generally non-refundable once delivered or consumed, except where Australian Consumer Law requires otherwise.

Refund decisions for discretionary goodwill requests are made by CasePath support after reviewing the account, payment record, and circumstances described in your request.

3. Australian Consumer Law

If you are a consumer under the Australian Consumer Law (ACL), you may have rights that cannot be excluded, including consumer guarantees that services will be provided with due care and skill and will be reasonably fit for any disclosed purpose.

Nothing in this Refund Policy, our Terms of Use, or any other CasePath document excludes, restricts, or modifies those non-excludable rights.

Where a failure to comply with a consumer guarantee is a major failure, you may be entitled to cancel the service and obtain a refund for the unused portion, or other remedies available under the ACL. Where a failure is not major, CasePath may remedy the failure by repairing or replacing the service within a reasonable time.

This section is a general statement of how CasePath approaches ACL obligations. It is not legal advice about your specific rights.

4. Cancellation process

You may cancel a subscription at any time. Cancellation stops future renewals; it does not automatically create a refund for the current period.

To cancel:

  1. Sign in to your CasePath account.
  2. Open billing or account settings and cancel the active subscription, or use the Stripe customer billing portal link if CasePath provides one for your account.
  3. If you cannot access self-serve cancellation, contact support via Contact with the email address on your account and the approximate purchase date.

After cancellation, your account and stored preparation data remain available according to our retention practices described in the Privacy Policy and Pricing FAQs (including any post-cancellation export window that applies to your plan).

5. Billing disputes

If you believe you were charged incorrectly, charged twice, or charged after a valid cancellation:

  1. Contact CasePath support first via Contact and select the billing category. Include your account email, approximate charge date, amount, and a short description of the issue.
  2. Allow a reasonable time for CasePath to investigate with Stripe payment records.
  3. If the charge is confirmed as incorrect, CasePath will arrange a refund or correction through Stripe.

You may also raise a dispute with your card issuer. We encourage contacting CasePath first so we can resolve genuine errors quickly. Chargebacks for correctly billed, delivered services may result in account review under our Terms of Use.

6. Contact process

For refund requests, cancellation help, or billing disputes:

  • Use the Contact form and choose the billing or account category.
  • Include the email address linked to your CasePath account.
  • Include the payment date, amount (AUD), and Stripe receipt or last four digits of the card if available.
  • Describe the outcome you are seeking (cancellation confirmation, refund, or billing correction).

We aim to acknowledge billing enquiries within two business days. Complex investigations may take longer where payment-provider records must be reviewed.
