Quick Start Guide

A 15–20 minute guided journey into CasePath.

Concise steps with links to the existing authoritative pages — not a second copy of those guides.

  2. How It Works
  3. Quick Start Guide

  1. 1

    Welcome

    CasePath helps you organise, prepare and understand family law processes. It does not replace a lawyer.

    This Quick Start is a guided journey. Each step links to the existing page that holds the full explanation.

    What CasePath can and cannot do · Our Mission · How It Works

  2. 2

    Create your account

    Create a free account to access your private workspace.

    Start signup · Details: How It Works — Create Your Account

  3. 3

    Verify your email

    Complete email verification when prompted during signup so your account remains secured to you.

    If you need help signing in afterwards, use Contact & Support.

  4. 4

    Create your first case

    Open Your Case to create or continue a matter. Your case profile becomes the foundation for preparation tools and document assistants.

    Open Your Case · Overview: Build Your Case Profile

  5. 5

    Complete your profile

    Add court details, children, other parties, important dates, existing orders and support people when you have them. Enter once; reuse across tools.

    Your Case workspace · Preparation Hub

  6. 6

    Upload documents

    Store and organise matter documents in Document Centre. For drafting aids and worksheets, use Court Documents and Court Document Assistants.

    Document Centre · Court Documents · Court Document Assistants

  7. 7

    Organise your dashboard

    Use Your Case to keep dates, documents, participants, notes and tasks connected to the matter.

    Your Case · Calendar Preview · Checklists

  8. 8

    Use the Document Centre

    Document Centre is the operational home for matter documents. Official court forms are available through the Official Forms Catalogue in the workspace when you need filing-oriented templates.

    Document Centre · Official Forms: Workspace — Official Forms Catalogue

  9. 9

    Prepare for mediation

    Organise issues, concerns and proposals before family dispute resolution.

    Mediation preparation · Mediation Preparation checklist · Prepare hub

  10. 10

    Prepare for court

    Use Court Day Preparation for day-of practical readiness, the Self-Represented Guide for process expectations, and checklists for filing and hearing steps.

    Court Day Preparation Beta · Self-Represented Guide · What List Is My Case In?

  11. 11

    Getting help

    Support Tools, Mental Health, Contact, and external helplines are available when you need people and services — not more product pages.

    Support Tools · Mental Health · Contact · Glossary · User Manual
