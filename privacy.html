Trust & transparency

Privacy Policy

How CasePath collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information connected with platform access, preparation workflows, uploaded materials, and AI-assisted features.

Information only — not legal advice.

At a glance: CasePath is designed to help users organise and prepare family law information securely and responsibly. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · Document version: Privacy-2026-07-27

1. Overview

Operating entity: THE LAYNA COMPANY PTY LIMITED (ABN 79 691 036 236) trading as KEDDEH SYSTEMS, operator of casepath.com.au. Chief Architect: Aboudy Keddeh (<aboudy@keddeh.com>). Privacy enquiries can be made via our Contact page.

CasePath is an Australian family-law preparation platform built around private-by-default workflows. People use it to organise chronologies, store documents, prepare drafts, and work through procedural steps during separation and court-related matters.

CasePath is not designed as a social platform or public content network, and it is not intended for routine human review of private vault materials.

You may upload sensitive personal information about yourself, your children, and your matter. We take privacy and security seriously, and we describe our practices here in plain language.

This policy applies when you:

  • create or use a CasePath account
  • use preparation workflows, checklists, and document tools
  • upload or enter materials in your workspace
  • use AI-assisted features described in our AI Use Disclosure
  • contact us for support or feedback

Where Australian privacy law applies to our handling of personal information, we aim to meet those obligations. This policy is not a substitute for legal advice about your own rights.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use CasePath, we may collect the following categories of information.

Account information

  • name
  • email address
  • authentication and account security details (for example, sign-in activity managed through our identity provider)

Platform usage and workspace content

  • chronology entries and timeline notes
  • uploaded evidence and documents
  • notes, drafts, and generated documents you create or store
  • workflow activity needed to operate features you use (for example, saving, organising, or retrieving your materials)

Payment and billing

  • subscription or payment status
  • billing-related identifiers needed to manage your plan

Payments are processed by Stripe. CasePath does not store full payment card numbers on its own systems.

Technical information

  • browser and device information
  • security and access logs
  • error and diagnostic information used to improve reliability
  • timestamps connected with sign-in and platform use

Support communications

  • emails or messages you send us
  • bug reports and feedback
  • information you choose to include when requesting help

3. How information is used

CasePath uses personal information to operate the platform and support your preparation workflows. This may include:

  • providing platform functionality you request
  • supporting account access and authentication
  • generating or organising documents and chronology information you work on
  • improving platform stability, performance, and security
  • delivering AI-assisted features (see section 4)
  • responding to support requests
  • investigating suspected misuse, fraud, or security threats where reasonably necessary (see section 6)

CasePath is not built around behavioural advertising or selling personal information to data brokers.

We do not sell personal information.

4. AI-assisted processing

Some CasePath features use AI-assisted systems to help with preparation tasks. When you use those features, information you enter or upload may be processed to:

  • summarise or structure content you provide
  • organise chronology entries and related notes
  • assist with drafting or formatting documents you are preparing
  • improve search and navigation within your materials
  • provide general procedural or educational information connected with your workflow
Please review AI outputs carefully. Automated outputs may be inaccurate, incomplete, or outdated. You should independently verify anything important before relying on it or filing documents.

Only upload information you are comfortable storing and processing for the task at hand. For more detail on how AI features work and their limits, see our AI Use Disclosure.

When you use Ask a Question, the text you submit in that request is sent to OpenAI (model used for this feature: gpt-4o-mini via OpenAI’s API) to generate a response. That processing is likely to involve overseas servers. Do not paste information you are not comfortable sending to that provider. CasePath does not intend AI requests to silently attach your vault files; only the content you enter for that request is sent.

We do not describe proprietary model architectures on this page; our focus is practical transparency about what happens to your information when you use these tools. See also our AI Use Disclosure.

5. Data storage and security

CasePath is hosted on modern cloud infrastructure with technical safeguards intended to protect personal information, including:

  • encryption in transit (TLS) between your browser and our services
  • encryption at rest for stored platform data, as provided by our infrastructure
  • authenticated access controls and account-based permissions
  • private or signed access patterns for stored files where applicable
  • security monitoring and ongoing hardening efforts

We work to reduce risk, but no online platform can guarantee absolute security. You should also protect your account credentials and devices, and be thoughtful about what you upload.

6. Platform protection and limited access

CasePath is designed to support private preparation and organisation workflows. The platform is not intended for routine human review of private user materials such as vault uploads, evidence files, chronology entries, or other preparation content you store for your matter.

However, CasePath may investigate suspected misuse of the platform, security threats, unlawful activity, technical abuse, or serious breaches of our terms where reasonably necessary to:

  • protect platform security
  • respond to abuse or safety reports
  • comply with legal obligations
  • maintain platform reliability and integrity, or
  • provide technical support you have requested

Where limited operational access to information is required, access should be restricted to what is reasonably necessary in the circumstances. This does not mean CasePath routinely reads, monitors, or moderates private vault materials.

Automated systems may also help detect technical abuse or security anomalies without routine human inspection of your preparation content. For related terms, see Terms of Use (section 9).

7. Analytics and tracking

CasePath uses privacy-focused analytics tools (including Plausible) to understand how the platform is used, measure reliability, and improve performance. These tools are not used to build behavioural advertising profiles.

We do not intentionally send private vault contents to analytics providers. Analytics generally relate to site usage patterns rather than the substance of your uploaded materials.

8. Data retention

While your account is active, workspace data and uploaded materials generally remain available so you can continue your preparation work.

If you delete content, close an account, or request deletion, removal from live systems may not be immediate. Copies may persist for a limited period in:

  • backups
  • security, fraud-prevention, or operational logs
  • records we are required or reasonably need to keep for security, dispute resolution, or legal compliance

Retention periods can vary depending on the type of record and operational needs. We aim to delete or de-identify information when it is no longer reasonably required. Specific retention periods are under internal review and are not listed as fixed commitments on this page until approved.

9. Third-party services and overseas processing

CasePath relies on service providers to operate. Depending on the feature you use, personal information may be processed by providers including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, storage, and Edge Functions
  • Cloudflare — content delivery, security controls, and Turnstile bot protection
  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Resend — transactional email delivery for some support and notification messages
  • Plausible Analytics — privacy-focused usage analytics
  • OpenAI — Ask a Question when you submit a prompt

CasePath’s primary Supabase database and storage tenancy is in Oceania (Sydney) (verified 27 July 2026). Some providers still operate infrastructure outside Australia, or may allow overseas access for support and security (for example AI, payments, email, CDN and analytics). See also Your Data. We do not claim that all personal information is stored only in Australia.

These providers process information as part of delivering the service. Their own privacy and security practices also apply.

10. Important limitations

CasePath is a preparation and information platform. It is important to understand the boundaries:

  • CasePath is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice.
  • Information you share with CasePath is not protected by legal professional privilege in the way communications with your own lawyer may be.
  • You should exercise care when uploading third-party information or highly sensitive material, including information about children or other people.

If you need confidential legal advice, speak with a qualified family lawyer, Legal Aid, or a community legal centre about your situation.

11. Your choices, access, and correction

You can:

  • review and edit much of your workspace and case profile information while signed in
  • manage or delete content you have uploaded within the platform, where those tools are available
  • contact us to request access to personal information we hold about you, correction of inaccurate information, account closure, or to raise a privacy question
  • stop using the platform at any time

You can start account deletion from Account Security in the signed-in app (or request help through Contact). Deletion is staged on CasePath servers: after confirmation, account access is restricted while approved workspace records and user-owned files (including Secure Vault objects and feedback screenshots linked to your account) are inventoried and removed, then the sign-in account is removed. The process is automatic and auditable; temporary failures are retried. Previously downloaded or externally shared copies cannot be recalled. Some operational or legally required records may be retained. Copies may remain for a period in backups, security logs, email provider systems, payment provider records, or AI provider systems as described in section 8. We will not claim that every historical copy worldwide is instantly erased.

When contacting us about privacy, include the email address you use to sign in and describe what you are asking us to do (for example: access, correction, or deletion).

12. Child safety and emergency matters

CasePath is not an emergency service and cannot respond to crises in real time.

If you or a child are in immediate danger, call emergency services on 000.

You can also contact 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732), Legal Aid in your state or territory, or a community legal centre.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as CasePath features, providers, or legal requirements evolve. When we make material changes, we will publish the updated version on this page with a revised “last updated” date.

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · Version: Privacy-2026-07-27

14. Contact and complaints

For privacy questions, access or correction requests, deletion requests, or complaints about how your information is handled, contact us through our Contact page. Please include enough detail for us to identify your account (such as the email address you use to sign in) and describe what you are asking us to do.

We aim to acknowledge privacy requests during Public Beta business hours and to respond within a reasonable period. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au). Whether particular Privacy Act complaint pathways apply to CasePath depends on how Australian privacy law applies to our organisation — that legal characterisation is separately under review.

This Privacy Policy is an information document about CasePath’s practices. It is not legal advice, and it is not a certification or approval by the OAIC.
