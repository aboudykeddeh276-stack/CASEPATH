YOUR DATA. YOUR PRIVACY. YOUR CONTROL.

Family law matters involve sensitive information about you, your children, and your case. This page explains where your information is stored, how it is protected, who can access it, and what you control.

  • Where your information is stored
  • How your information is protected
  • Who can access your information
  • Backups and recovery
  • What you can control
  • Secure Vault (in active development)

This page explains how your information is handled, including what we have verified and what is still being confirmed. Information only — not legal advice.

Why this matters

Family law often involves some of the most personal information people will ever store online.

  • Medical reports.
  • Children’s information.
  • Court documents.
  • Personal journals.
  • Evidence.

You deserve to understand how that information is protected, based on verified infrastructure and published policies.

How to read this page: Status labels show what is live today versus what is planned. Verified ✓ means we can confirm this from current application code, configuration, or infrastructure records. Planned means under development, not yet generally available. In progress means work has started but is not complete. See the verification note at the bottom.

Last updated: July 2026

1. Privacy Promise

  • Verified ✓ CasePath is built for private family-law preparation — not public sharing, behavioural advertising, or selling your personal information.
  • Verified ✓ This page is a transparency document. It describes how your information is handled — not marketing copy.
  • Verified ✓ Private workspace materials (vault uploads, evidence files, chronology entries) are not intended for routine human review by CasePath staff.

For collection, use, retention, and your rights, see our Privacy Policy.

2. Your Information

Depending on how you use CasePath, we may store:

Verified ✓ Account

Email address, sign-in sessions, account status, and plan access.

Verified ✓ Billing

Subscription status and payment records. Card numbers are handled by Stripe — not stored on CasePath.

Verified ✓ Case data

Case profiles, chronology, checklists, and preparation progress when you use signed-in workspace features and they are available for your account.

Verified ✓ Documents

Drafts and documents you create or save through platform tools. Some standalone tools may also use device-local storage.

Verified ✓ Uploads

Files you attach through your workspace vault are stored in private, access-controlled cloud storage linked to your account. File uploads are encrypted in your browser before upload when Secure Vault is set up.

Verified ✓ Support requests

Messages and contact details you submit through the contact form.

3. Where Your Information Is Stored

Your signed-in account, database and file storage for CasePath’s primary backend are hosted on Supabase in the Oceania (Sydney) region (verified from the live project configuration on 27 July 2026). Other providers used for delivery, security, payments, email, analytics or AI may process information in other countries — see the Privacy Policy.

Verified ✓ Account & case data

Stored in an encrypted cloud database, accessible through your signed-in account under row-level security. Authorised operators may access systems for support and security.

Verified ✓ Uploaded files

Stored in a private file vault. Files are not publicly listed or browsable.

Verified ✓ Sign-in & email

Authentication and account recovery emails are handled through our auth provider.

Verified ✓ Public pages

Informational pages (like this one) are served as static web pages.

4. How Your Information Is Protected

Current protections include:

  • Verified ✓ Encrypted connections — Your browser connects to CasePath and our backend over HTTPS (TLS). Edge hardening (e.g. HSTS) is being verified with hosting.
  • Verified ✓ Account sign-in — Email verification, password reset, and session management through our auth provider.
  • Verified ✓ Access controls — Your workspace data is scoped to your account. Other users cannot access your case by default. If you use Lawyer Collaboration, access you grant to your lawyer is an exception.
  • Verified ✓ Private file storage — Uploads sit in a private vault with time-limited download links.
  • Verified ✓ Encryption at rest — Database and metadata rely on provider encryption at rest. Vault files use browser-side encryption when Secure Vault is enabled. CasePath does not add a separate encryption layer for standard workspace data today.
  • Verified ✓ Bot protection — Cloudflare Turnstile helps reduce automated abuse on sign-up and authentication.
  • Verified ✓ Multi-factor authentication (TOTP) — Optional authenticator-app MFA via Supabase Auth. Supported apps include Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, Bitwarden, and other standard TOTP apps. Recovery codes are available as a backup.
What we do not claim today: CasePath does not currently offer platform-wide end-to-end encryption, zero-knowledge storage for all data, or a generally available Secure Vault product. Vault file encryption is available after setup; the full Secure Vault product remains Planned.

5. Who Can Access Your Information

You

Verified ✓ When signed in, you can access your own case data, documents, and vault uploads through your account.

Verified ✓ If you use Lawyer Collaboration, you control what your connected lawyer can access. Sharing is initiated by you, not by CasePath.

CasePath operators

Verified ✓ A small number of authorised administrators can access account metadata (email, plan, credits) for support, billing, and security operations.

Verified ✓ With server-side access, operators could read stored file contents in principle for standard storage. Secure Vault ciphertext is not readable without your keys. CasePath policy is that private workspace materials are not intended for routine human review.

Limited access may occur for security incidents, abuse reports, legal obligations, or support you request — as described in the Privacy Policy.

Secure Vault (in active development)

Planned Secure Vault is intended to encrypt selected uploads in your browser before they reach storage, with keys held only by you. When available, the design goal is that CasePath operators should not be able to decrypt those files without your vault passphrase and recovery key.

In progress Core encryption path is live for vault uploads; full product documentation and general availability are not complete.

6. Secure Vault (Planned)

Current Protection vs Secure Vault

Planned feature

Secure Vault encryption is available for vault uploads after setup. The full Secure Vault product is not generally available today. This comparison explains what you have now versus what is planned.

Topic Current protection (today) Secure Vault (planned)
Who can access data Verified ✓ You, plus authorised CasePath operators with server-side access in limited circumstances. Secure Vault ciphertext is not readable without your keys. Planned You hold the keys. CasePath operators should not be able to decrypt vault files without your passphrase and recovery key.
Recovery options Verified ✓ Reset your account password via email. Restores access to your workspace under standard controls. Planned Separate vault passphrase and recovery key. Losing both means encrypted files cannot be recovered.
Privacy level Verified ✓ Strong account isolation and private storage. Operators could read standard file bytes in principle; Secure Vault ciphertext is not readable without your keys. Planned Stronger confidentiality for selected uploads — CasePath stores encrypted data it cannot read.
Encryption model Verified ✓ TLS in transit + cloud-provider encryption at rest. Browser-side encryption for vault files when Secure Vault is set up. Planned Browser-side encryption before upload, with user-held keys.
Support implications Verified ✓ CasePath can help with account access and standard workspace recovery. Planned CasePath cannot recover lost vault keys or decrypt files for you.

Standard protection (today)

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
  • Account-scoped access controls
  • Private vault storage with signed links
  • Cloud encryption at rest
  • Browser-side encryption for vault files (when Secure Vault is set up)

7. Backups & Recovery

Account recovery (today)

  • Verified ✓ Reset your password using email recovery links to regain access to your account and workspace.
  • Verified ✓ If you lose access to your email account, password recovery may not be possible until you regain that email.

Infrastructure backups

To be confirmed CasePath relies on cloud-provider backup practices. Specific backup frequency, retention, and restore procedures will be published here once verified.

Verified ✓ Signed-in users can start staged account deletion from Account Security. Approved account records and user-owned storage objects are removed through a server-side job; previously downloaded or shared copies cannot be recalled. The Privacy Policy notes that some copies may persist for a limited period in backups and operational systems.

Secure Vault recovery (planned)

Planned Secure Vault is intended to use a separate recovery key. If you lose both your vault passphrase and recovery key, encrypted files would not be recoverable — even by CasePath.
