AI Use Disclosure

How CasePath uses AI-assisted systems to support preparation, organisation, procedural guidance, and educational information for self-represented users.

Information only — not legal advice.

Important: CasePath provides informational and preparation support tools only. CasePath does not provide legal advice, legal representation, or personalised legal strategy.

What CasePath is

CasePath is a self-preparation and case organisation platform built for Australian family law workflows. It is designed to help you organise information, prepare documents, and understand court procedures at your own pace.

Some features use AI-assisted tools to make the platform easier to use — for example, helping you structure notes, navigate your materials, or follow procedural steps. These tools support your preparation; they do not act on your behalf or speak for you in court.

How AI features are used

CasePath may use AI-assisted systems to help with tasks such as:

  • explaining court processes in plain language
  • summarising information you have uploaded or entered
  • organising chronology entries and timelines
  • assisting with affidavit formatting and structure
  • helping structure parenting order drafts you prepare
  • surfacing procedural information relevant to your workflow
  • improving search and navigation within your case materials
Important: AI outputs are generated automatically. They may contain inaccuracies, omissions, or outdated information. Court rules and legislation can change. You must independently verify anything important before relying on it or filing documents.

Some AI-assisted content may not reflect legislative or procedural changes introduced after the source material was last updated.

What CasePath can help with

CasePath is built around preparation and organisation. Typical uses include:

Chronology preparation

Organise dates and events you enter so your timeline is easier to review and share with a lawyer if you choose.

Evidence organisation

Keep documents and notes grouped so you can find what you need during proceedings.

Document drafting assistance

Support structuring drafts you review and edit before any filing or service.

Procedural guidance

General information about common steps in Australian family law processes.

Affidavit formatting support

Help with layout and structure — not legal assessment of what to allege or prove.

Parenting order preparation

Assist with organising proposals and drafts for your own review.

Mediation preparation

Personal notes and checklists to help you prepare for discussions.

Educational information

Plain-language explanations of terms and processes — not tailored legal advice.

What CasePath cannot do

To be clear, CasePath does not:

  • provide legal advice
  • recommend legal strategy or litigation steps
  • predict court outcomes
  • determine the strength of your case
  • represent you in court or negotiations
  • replace a solicitor or barrister
  • make decisions for you
  • guarantee accuracy, acceptance of documents, or outcomes
Your responsibility: You remain responsible for all decisions, court filings, negotiations, and legal outcomes connected with your matter.

Your responsibilities

Using CasePath works best when you treat outputs as starting points for your own review. We encourage you to:

  • independently review all information and AI-generated content
  • verify court requirements with the relevant registry or rules
  • confirm filing and service obligations before acting
  • seek legal advice from a qualified lawyer or Legal Aid where appropriate
  • carefully review every generated document before filing or serving it

If something on the platform does not match your court’s rules or your circumstances, rely on official sources and professional advice — not the platform alone.

When to seek professional legal help

Some situations need a qualified family lawyer, duty lawyer, or urgent court assistance rather than self-preparation tools alone. Examples include:

  • family violence or safety concerns
  • child safety risks
  • relocation or travel disputes
  • urgent recovery or recovery-order matters
  • complex property or financial disputes
  • court representation or advocacy
  • settlement advice and binding agreements
  • litigation strategy and case management decisions

If you or your children are in immediate danger, contact emergency services on 000.

You can also contact 1800RESPECT (1800 737 732), Legal Aid in your state or territory, or a community legal centre.

Data and privacy

When you use AI-assisted features, text and materials you provide may be processed to deliver platform functionality. CasePath uses your data to operate the service — not to sell personal information.

Private materials are handled in line with our Privacy Policy. Only upload information you are comfortable storing for your preparation workflow, and avoid uploading sensitive details that are not necessary for the task at hand.

Reporting issues

If you notice a problem, please tell us so we can review it. You can report:

  • inaccurate or misleading AI outputs
  • formatting or document structure issues
  • procedural information that seems inconsistent with public sources
  • technical faults or accessibility barriers

Contact us via Contact. For privacy-related questions, see Privacy.
