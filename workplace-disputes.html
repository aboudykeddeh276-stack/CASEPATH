Context-preserving legal preparation

One matter. Four planes that never collapse.

The architecture preserves the user’s source material while progressively organising it for legal review. An account does not become evidence, evidence does not become a legal conclusion, and a legal possibility does not become an authorised action.

01

Source plane

Preserve the user’s expression, dates, people, records and requested outcomes without silently rewriting them.

02

Evidence plane

Link propositions to supporting, contradicting and missing material while retaining provenance and uncertainty.

03

Legal plane

Present possible legal lenses and current official sources conditionally; application remains for the qualified lawyer.

04

Action plane

Prepare pathway questions, document drafts and a lawyer handover without filing, representing or promising an outcome.

The line we do not cross

Software can prepare the record. A lawyer must assess the law.

Clear boundaries make the pack more useful. CasePath labels what came from you, what is supported by a record, what remains unverified and what requires professional judgment.

  • 01

    No solicitor–client relationshipUsing this site does not retain a lawyer or create legal professional privilege.

  • 02

    No legal conclusionA polished document is not proof that a fact, claim, defence, remedy or amount is legally valid.

  • 03

    No filing authorityNothing is sent to a court, tribunal, police agency, opponent or lawyer by CasePath.

  • 04

    No deadline assuranceUrgent time limits can apply. Verify them immediately with a lawyer or the relevant official body.
